# Apex Regime and Strategy Matrix

This matrix records intended eligibility for validation. Existing strategy gates
remain authoritative until shadow evidence supports a change.

| Family | Preferred context | Prohibited/conditional context | Primary confirmation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Momentum breakout | Expansion after valid structure | Unconfirmed compression or late chase | Closed breakout plus participation |
| Breakout continuation | Accepted structural break | Failed break or direct HTF opposition | Acceptance and continuation room |
| Breakout retest | Confirmed break with orderly retest | Retest failure | Retest hold/reclaim |
| First pullback continuation | Fresh directional impulse | Exhaustion or deep structural failure | First controlled pullback |
| Trend pullback | Established trend and structure | Range/chop or broken trend | Structural hold |
| Compression expansion | Mature compression | Premature direction assignment | Closed expansion trigger |
| Range reversal | Stable range edge | Accepted breakout | Edge rejection/reclaim |
| Failed breakout reversal | Rejected break | Accepted continuation | Recross and failure confirmation |
| Liquidity rejection reversal | Structural sweep area | Unconfirmed wick alone | Sweep plus reclaim |
| VWAP reclaim/rejection | Intraday structure alignment | VWAP-only direction | Closed reclaim/rejection |
| Momentum scalp | Short-horizon alignment | Critical HTF opposition or chase | Execution-frame trigger |
| Exhaustion reversal | Mature exhaustion with structure | Momentum oscillator alone | Failure plus opposite confirmation |

Derivatives data modifies participation confidence; it does not independently set
direction.
